My current research interests lie in natural language processing (NLP), particularly cross-lingual text generation and its applications to patent drafting. Selected publications

INLG 2026 · Accepted

A Large-Scale Dataset for Cross-Lingual Multi-Jurisdiction Patent Drafting from Patent Cooperation Treaty Applications

Trung Phuong Le, Hirofumi Nonaka, Koji Marusaki, Katsuhito Sudoh, Seiya Kawano

International Natural Language Generation Conference · The Netherlands

Accepted · Publication link coming soon.

Research summary

We study the generation of patent claim sets for a target language, jurisdiction, and examination stage. Using 884,179 patent families linked through Patent Cooperation Treaty applications, we build a Japanese–English dataset connecting Japanese and US patent records. The benchmark evaluates machine translation, prompted language models, and supervised adaptation for drafting claims that reflect the target patent office and examination stage.

Journal article · 2025

Neural network translations for building SentiWordNets

Khang Nhut Lam, Trung Phuong Le, Khanh Cong Ngu, Kien Trung Le, Phuc Minh Le, Huy Hoang-Dang Nguyen, Jugal Kalita

Journal of Intelligent Information Systems, 63, 1033–1054 (2025). Springer.

Publisher ↗DOI ↗
Research summary

This work uses neural machine translation to construct SentiWordNets: lexical resources that associate word senses with sentiment scores. It proposes ways to select translated synset members and assign their sense order, helping build sentiment resources for languages supported by machine translation.
