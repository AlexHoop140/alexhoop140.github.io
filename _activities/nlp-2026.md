I presented a poster at NLP2026 in Utsunomiya, Japan, on our work **A Cross-Lingual Multi-Jurisdiction Dataset for Patent Drafting from Patent Cooperation Treaty Applications**.

The work explores patent drafting across Japanese and English using patent applications linked through the Patent Cooperation Treaty.

[Read the meeting paper (PDF)](https://www.anlp.jp/proceedings/annual_meeting/2026/pdf_dir/C9-21.pdf) · Presentation C9-21. Published in the annual meeting proceedings without peer review.
